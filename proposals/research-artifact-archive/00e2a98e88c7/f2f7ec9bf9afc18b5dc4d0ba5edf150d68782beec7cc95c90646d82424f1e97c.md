## Independent source-to-claim check

All13 original cited primary sources were independently opened. The following gives claim scope rather than copying source recipes. Numerical commercial values remain unadopted.

H1 supports completed modular parts, screw/cable/BTB assembly and model-specific mechanical/electrical/firmware compatibility. Its current repair instructions also mention a controlled antistatic environment for the core module. This supports inventory/hierarchy obligations, not original manufacture or flagship equivalence. H2 supports permitted USB Type-C negotiation signaling and connector certification requirements within USB-IF's program, not this sink circuit or a frozen charging target. H3 supports an authorized-lab workflow and checking additional operator-specific requirements; it does not establish that every carrier requires PTCRB. [Fairphone](https://support.fairphone.com/hc/en-us/articles/18744611991825-Fairphone-5-Replace-a-spare-part), [USB-IF](https://compliance.usb.org/index.asp?Format=Standard&UpdateFile=USBC), [PTCRB](https://www.ptcrb.com/get-certified/).

E1 describes antenna splits co-molded into a particular aluminum tablet enclosure and CNC finishing. It is valid precedent for a conditional RF integration option, not a mandatory phone insert or adopted geometry/alloy. E2 discusses commercial milling stability/workholding and chip evacuation; no manual-machine or cutting-fluid choice follows from it. These support R02's caution and required operation capability. [Apple enclosure](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101900), [Sandvik milling](https://www.sandvik.coromant.com/en-gb/milling-appl-guide).

C1 supports moisture-related visible defects and hidden polymer-chain degradation; its material/grade distinctions matter. Pages2–5 also describe drying cabinets for small batches, several dryer arrangements and a simple TVI test limited to unreinforced Makrolon/Apec. TVI screens sufficient/insufficient dryness; it is not a quantitative laboratory moisture measurement. The brochure distinguishes low-moisture laboratory methods from simple weighing and should not be treated as a current universal weighing-instrument performance limit. Add these conditional simpler options to R03; no material selection, setpoint or target threshold is adopted. C2 supports matching machine size to material, shot/mold/clamp demands and mold heating/cooling capability; it does not qualify the project's manual apparatus. [Covestro drying](https://solutions.covestro.com/-/media/covestro/solution-center/whitepapers/injection-molding-of-high-quality-molded-parts-drying.pdf), [Covestro equipment](https://www.covestro.com/-/media/covestro/solution-center/brands/downloads/imported/1556891051.pdf).

G1 supports potassium/sodium ion exchange and distinct cover/substrate functions. It does not provide our composition, dwell or process recipe; its approximate bath temperature remains context. G2 supports controlled edge-damage introduction followed by four-point bending. Its study uses an air/belt-slide arrangement, defined contact insert, force sensor and velocity photogates. These are one supported implementation, not automatically mandatory features of every acceptable earlier fixture. A different implementation needs its own evidence, reference transfer and geometry/control qualification. Preserve separate dynamic-contact and bending subassemblies/duties and destructive specimens under R05. [Corning FAQ](https://www.corning.com/gorillaglass/worldwide/en/faqs.html), [Corning edge study](https://www.corning.com/microsites/csm/gorillaglass/PI_Sheets/COR_GG_WhtPaper_Controlled_Edge.pdf).

G3 documents optical-waveguide stress measurement, a physical PC/software host, CS/DOL ranges and different wavelength variants. It advertises cross-section stress distribution and double-ion-exchange features, so profile capability is not disproved. Its ranges depend on the glass's photoelastic constants and refractive-index distribution; quoted accuracy applies to standard glass. The catalog does not independently qualify full depth profile/interior tension in our unspecified glass or establish instrument construction/calibration. Make this precise in the glass identity request while retaining unmet demands. [Orihara FSM-6000X](https://www.orihara-ss.co.jp/catalog/photoe/FSM/FSM-6000X_E.pdf).

D1 supports vacuum organic evaporation with aligned fine metal masks and explicitly describes a different mask for each RGB color. It also places subsequent complementary layers/encapsulation on the same processed substrate. R08 should retain this installed mask set and its physical operation duties; a reticle-writing tool is not the resulting perforated metal mask. No pixel pitch, complete recipe or process yield is established. [Samsung FMM](https://global.samsungdisplay.com/30929).

D2 supports one rigid OLED glass-cover/frit/laser sequence; that commercial sequence also has seal preconditioning and UV hardening of an additional mother-glass seal. If this specific implementation is selected, preserve those duties or justify a distinct one-off cell-level alternative. D3 supports alternating inorganic/organic barrier layers in a flexible-panel example and particle/pinhole mitigation. Neither supports our rigid/TFE combination's thermal/chemical compatibility or lifetime, and neither establishes TFE cannot be used on rigid glass. R07 correctly keeps alternatives separate. [Samsung encapsulation](https://global.samsungdisplay.com/29626/), [Samsung TFE](https://global.samsungdisplay.com/29678?category=78&page=7&type=list).

## Hierarchy, inventory and alternatives

The reports distinguish installed modules from tools that manufacture them. Repeated substrate/FPC/material IDs are not automatically extra incorporated stock. R06 should produce an explicit state/quantity ledger: bare prepared glass, backplane-bearing glass, OLED-bearing intermediate and encapsulated panel; identify actual driver/FPC installation stage. If later schema revisions split states, create only meaningful independently understandable outputs, retain material/utility/tool obligations through the actual consumer, and keep the selected graph acyclic. A same-name edge deletion is insufficient evidence of allocation.

Operation inventory remains incomplete. Known open duties include assembly contacts/protected storage/waste; machining cutting/chip collection and qualified stock; cover feed conditioning/mold vents/ejection/thermal capacity; glass cut/edge/rinse/bath-contact tools, references, coupon stock and waste; display installed FMMs/carriers, process monitors, clean transfers, contacts, independent photometry and barrier-life tests. Whether these are supplied by a reusable platform or a distinct consumer is a quantity/capacity/availability decision. Construction/commissioning inventory never automatically replenishes a later production job's fuel, water, salt, feed, cutting compound or cleaning stock.

Modular screw/clip versus bonded final assembly, machined versus near-net metal versus polymer enclosure, molding versus sheet machining/compression forming, chemical versus thermal strengthening, and lid sealing versus TFE are coherent alternate methods. They remain hypotheses except for the narrow source precedents above. None is a proved shorter from-nature route or a capability-equivalent substitute. The newly cited small-batch drying/screening options provide a genuine simpler-route investigation while leaving grade-specific and low-moisture requirements open.
