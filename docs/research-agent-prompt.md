## Corrections from the first coordinated discovery pass

- Assign role by actual use and physical identity. Feedstocks and incorporated devices are Parts. Reusable ignition apparatus, firing beds, vessels, fixtures, comparison references and instruments are Tools. A reusable tool is not consumed merely because a generic helper defaulted its role to Part. Requirements must state incorporation, consumption, reuse or verification explicitly.
- Check canonical meaning, not the word alone. Handset electronic `storage` cannot represent a covered stockpile. A bench `audio-amplifier` cannot represent incorporated handset speaker-drive electronics without a compatible distinct route and demand. Keep a real reuse explanation for new grades or subassemblies.
- A low-tier wooden grip trial cannot serve a molten-metal pour, molten strengthening bath or ferrite furnace. Do not put an unproved property such as heat resistance into an item's title. Specify actual temperature, chemistry, mass, contact time and grip needs as unknown when unknown; retain incompatible earlier/advanced tiers separately.
- Physical inventories must match the chosen method. Washed fiber needs a vessel as well as water. A soda-lime glass route needs an explicit compatible calcium feed rather than generic ceramic stock. Firing needs fuel, ignition, forming, placement and handling. A populated board needs its mounted active chips, not just a list of loose chips in the handset's parent route.
- Keep route alternatives separate. Wirebond and flip-chip packages, SOI and polysilicon MEMS processes, loose copper pickup and heat-assisted bedrock extraction, dry fiber preparation and washing/retting have different physical obligations. Never combine their ingredients merely because all appear in a source or an older seed list.
- Replacing a selected hypothesis requires replacing its visible inventory and making outline consistently. Preserve older descriptions under explicit prior/provenance fields. Duplicate route IDs must be rejected or replaced deliberately, never silently repeated in an array.
- A declared acyclic frontier may still conceal the eventual first-tool loop. List the unresolved controller, power, metrology or seed-crystal prerequisites, and the blocking eventual dependency. Do not remove a known requirement to make the cycle test pass.
- MEMS devices need specified structural/sacrificial layers, anchors, release and stiction management, cavity sealing/feedthroughs, packaging protection and a coherent release/bond/dice sequence. A patterned silicon wafer or generic ASIC encapsulant is insufficient.
- Preserve manufacturing hierarchy. A final assembly consumes completed modules; its module routes consume their internal chips, sheets, films and subassemblies. Do not flatten every descendant back into the root as another incorporated input or list all upstream factory tools as final-assembly tools. When correcting a flat inventory, keep every known physical requirement reachable through the actual selected consumer; retain unresolved allocation explicitly rather than deleting it. Containment and repeated use are not additional consumed stock.
- Supply readable record metadata as well as graph schema: plain name, Part/Tool role, domain, short function/consumer summary, evidence label, unknowns and provenance. Frontier nodes need these too. A structural gate pass does not verify page-rendering fields or science. If no URL is available, preserve the reference as an unlinked lead; never count a null or invented URL as a checked source. Complete-record upserts must retain the display metadata of prior records.
