## Bounded BFS assignment and automatic loop inventory

The coordinator supplies the BFS expansion depth. The current user instruction is **three dependency hops for one root**: repeat the discovery steps for the parent (relative depth 0), every child (depth 1), then every grandchild (depth 2). Their requirements are added/reused as great-grandchildren (depth 3), which are not expanded in this assignment. Finish all outputs at one depth before the next. Existing selected inventories count as explored; inspect and reuse them rather than replacing them unnecessarily, but still traverse their requirements so undiscovered grandchildren are included. Deduplicate shared outputs at their shortest distance from this assignment's root. Skip explicit natural/external boundaries, bootstrap-queued items and another live owner's work; record skipped/blocked outputs briefly. Do not extend to another root or beyond the assigned depth. Earlier two-hop runs expanded only parent and children; the current three-hop run also expands grandchildren.

Before selecting requirements for each existing output, run `python -B scripts/agent_context.py inventory ITEM_ID --ids-only --output proposals/YOUR_RUN/inventory-ITEM_ID.json`. This reads the live complete canonical map and separates all IDs into `direct_parents`, `other_ancestors` and `non_ancestors`; search/full records supply names, aliases, functions and suitability when needed. Omit `--ids-only` only when full repeated records are actually useful. Never require the output itself or any `forbidden_requirement_ids`: those edges create loops. Refresh just before using an output's relationships after a commit changes the revision; do not rewrite inventories that will not be consulted again. A root's ancestor set is not its child's set. New pending identities need a staged identity check as well. The full shared add operation still checks combined drafts and the complete current graph.

## Steps

1. **List the manufacturing methods from memory.** Give a short list of plausible methods for the assigned output. Mark uncertain recollections; do not begin a literature review for every item.
2. **Decide which method moves toward raw materials quickest.** Prefer the plausible method with fewer layers of refined inputs, complex machinery, precision infrastructure and earlier utilities. This is a provisional judgment, not proof of a globally shortest route. Preserve the required function and capability; a coarse trial is a separate state rather than a silent substitute for a final part.
3. **List the Parts and Tools for that method.** Include incorporated components and feedstocks; consumed reagents, lubricants, abrasives, joining/coating materials and replacement stock; fabrication tools, dies, fixtures, supports and work surfaces; actual power, heat, ignition, water, gases, vacuum and cooling; containers, transfer, storage, cleaning, handling, protection and waste equipment; verification instruments and independent references; and physical control/programming hardware where needed. Reuse tool-bank names and existing Part records. Mark unknowns without inventing numerical limits.
4. **Try entering them in the map and see whether it fails due to creating a loop.** Submit the compact draft through scripts/add_discovery.py or its add_to_map function. Any discovery agent may call this shared add operation directly. It checks the complete current graph and commits atomically only when the addition is valid; a rejected loop leaves the map unchanged and returns its exact path. Accepted additions automatically refresh the map pages and tool-name bank. Missing IDs, duplicate identities and conflicting replacements are separate errors to correct. The coordinator reviews and coordinates work after insertion rather than approving each addition.
5. **Check the loops.** From memory, try doing those steps with other inputs/tools, genuinely earlier capability tiers or manual operation. Try another manufacturing method if needed to avoid the loop. Retain the complete changed inventory and rerun the gate. Do not delete a real requirement, rename the same unavailable tool, mix alternatives into one recipe or assume incompatible grades are interchangeable merely to pass the check.
6. **Escalate loops that cannot be quickly resolved from memory, then move on.** After a brief attempt, message the main agent with the item, failed method, exact cycle, alternatives tried and unresolved prerequisite. Preserve the complete failed candidate. Leave its construction frontier open and continue to the next item. The main agent creates a bootstrapping discovery specialist at xhigh effort, using bootstrap-discovery-agent-prompt.md. That specialist seeks a plausible escape from memory or light research, without a full research assignment.
7. **Repeat within the assigned depth.** In BFS mode, expand the parent, all children and all grandchildren in that order for the current three-hop assignment; add/reuse their immediate requirements and stop before expanding great-grandchildren. Traverse existing inventories to include their descendants and deduplicate shared outputs. A named nonterminal output with no inventory still needs discovery. Follow a different depth only if explicitly assigned by the coordinator/user. In DFS mode, choose one of the more complex undiscovered children and follow that branch, preserving all side requirements. Coordinate shared children rather than duplicating another agent's work.

## Reminders

- Order each page's immediate Parts/Tools approximately as **major Part → major Tool → minor Part → minor Tool**. Importance is relative to the parent output: a display is a major handset part; small assembly aids come later. Keep this order in the submitted requires rows, since the map uses it for parent grouping. Preserve every requirement and its correct incorporated/consumed/reused/verification classification. Existing pages are treated as already ordered; improve their order as they are next revised, without wholesale graph changes.

- Reusable tools are Tools; incorporated machine components and consumed feedstocks are Parts. Record incorporated, consumed, reused or verification use. Expendable moulds are consumed tooling.
- Electronic storage is not stockpile storage. A bench amplifier is not a phone's incorporated driver. A wooden grip trial is not molten-metal/salt handling equipment. Check real function and capability before reusing a name.
- Washing needs a vessel as well as water; firing needs fuel, ignition and handling; a populated board needs mounted active chips; packaging needs actual packaging materials. Do one known-requirement sweep without repetitive boilerplate for every child.
- Keep one coherent recipe per selection. Wirebond/flip-chip, SOI/polysilicon, dry/wet preparation and alternative extraction methods are separate choices.
- Distinguish natural occurrence, collected stock, prepared feed and qualified output. No natural endpoint is approved merely because a trail reaches an occurrence candidate.
- Preserve hierarchy: modules consume their internal components; the final parent does not consume the same parts again. Changed methods must update their inventory and outline consistently.

Save compact batches containing methods considered, provisional choice and reason, immediate Parts/Tools with IDs and uses, new identities/reuse candidates, loop-check results and preserved failed alternatives. Routine provisional IDs may be submitted together without waiting for individual approval. The shared add operation enriches schema fields, checks and saves valid changes, and refreshes the view. The coordinator routes disputed identities to the owning agents, spot-checks saved outcomes and assigns research. Technical discovery, research and corrections remain with agents. Research agents investigate selected unresolved issues separately. User-facing completion stays brief; map additions are the report.

## Feedback from the first five researched items — 9 October 2026

After the immediate inventory, make one short memory-first known-obligation sweep. This is not a request for a literature review for every discovery item. The independently reviewed five-item reports and exact examples are in `proposals/2026-10-09-continuation/research-review/section01/review.md` and `research-bfs/section02/discovery-feedback.md`.

- State the actual input/output material condition and unresolved caller grade. Generic aluminum, polymer, water, seals or a named instrument do not establish composition, temper, moisture, cleanliness or target duty. Create distinct states only when there is a meaningful physical construction/capability difference.
- Allocate production-operation stock separately from building and commissioning the reusable apparatus: feed, fuel, coolant, salt, cleaning stock, wear/replacement material, trial/reject/destructive specimens and waste. Existing transitive stock definitions do not establish the quantity or availability for this job.
- Name the property being accepted and the actual measurement/reference function. A microscope/length instrument does not automatically measure glass stress, room dew point does not establish pellet moisture, and a generic pull fixture does not establish glass edge-impact/four-point-bend capability. Unknown range, sensitivity, accuracy and suitability stay explicit.
- Track the physical object advancing through intermediate states and quantities. Repeated sheet/FPC IDs can denote the same workpiece, installed contents, another consumed unit or reusable contact; determine allocation before removing or moving an edge. Preserve every known requirement reachable through its actual consumer.
- Distinguish finished installed process tooling and consumables from the equipment that makes them. An RGB fine-metal-mask method needs its actual color-pattern mask set and carrier/alignment stock; a reticle writer is not the finished mask.
- Keep selected alternatives coherent and preserve evidence scope. A commercial feature, repair instruction or another material/geometry is limited precedent. It is not qualification of our capability or proof of a complete inventory.

Label a reported gap as a declared-inventory fact, a method-supported obligation, or an unresolved design/suitability decision. An absent consumer allocation is not proof that its item is absent everywhere in the graph. Route concrete corrections to the assigned physical owner; do not silently overwrite shared selections or discard required acceptance to pass the gate.
